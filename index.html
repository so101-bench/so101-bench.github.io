SO-101 Bench

Accessible Spatial Manipulation

Truman Hickok · Independent · hickoktruman@gmail.com

Paper ↗Code ↗Live leaderboard ↗Data ↓

Low-cost arms like the SO-101 are now the most common entry point to robot learning, yet no released policy performs spatially constrained, language-conditioned tasks on them out of the box. SO-101 Bench is a simulated benchmark built on a digital twin of a real SO-101 setup: four tasks, 138 objects in three generalization splits and 916 evaluation episodes. Policies trained only on its simulated data keep their ranking on the real robot, and run out of the box in unseen real environments with unseen objects.

SO-101 Bench

Four tasks, scored as five families: bin (one or four objects), next to, between, and directional move.

Primary-evaluation demonstrations · Four policies · All successful · 1×

916 primary-evaluation episodes span 15 task-family × object-split cells. ROBUST: 178 held-out appearance episodes; PLAN: 48 rotation-to-fit episodes; MEM: 51 occlusion episodes. Subsets overlap.

Learned policies receive 36 hours of fine-tuning on 4 × A100-80GB GPUs; checkpoints are selected on 39 held-out validation episodes.

Real-Only vs. Sim-Only Policy Correspondence

Independently trained NVIDIA GR00T N1.6-3B policies are compared on 906 episodes matched by task, instruction and ordered object set. Across 15 task × split conditions, Pearson r = 0.86 and Spearman ρ = 0.74.

Sim2Real with No Real Data

Five policies that never saw real data from our setup are evaluated with identical weights in simulation and on the real robot, on 45 matched layouts: four learned policies fine-tuned only on simulated demonstrations, and GPT-6.1 Sol with PyRUA-Lean, which hands grasping to the same sim-trained Galaxea 0.5. The ranking of all five is preserved: Spearman ρ = 1.00; MMRV = 0.000 [95% CI 0.000, 0.169]; Pearson r = 0.90. The agent also leads on the real robot, 64% (29/45) against 53% (24/45) for Galaxea 0.5 alone.

Real-robot wall-clock comparison. On the same 45 layouts, median elapsed time to rollout stop is 197 seconds for Sol, versus 38 for Galaxea 0.5, 38 for GR00T N1.7, 59 for π0.5 and 56 for MolmoAct2-SO101. Both successes and failures are included.

LLM Agents: Harness and Model Ablations

Ablations use 60 fixed primary-evaluation episodes: 30 easy and 30 hard, selected before running the agents. With GPT-6.1 Sol, success rises from 35% with direct tool calls to 67% with PyRUA-Lean and 85% with the Galaxea 0.5 hand-off; Galaxea 0.5 alone scores 53%.
